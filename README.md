*An extensive vanilla+ overhaul of the swamps with more foliage, new blocks, new creatures and many secrets to discover!*

![Swamp](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/fd6218baa952b2d09d83f4dbd548930adcbfa119.png)

Swamps are now more dense and boggy with an increase in plant life and _mud!_

Unique foliage can now be found in swamps which are as follows:

![Purple Heart Woodset](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/08e65517993ea177d7e29ac0af7319e1c43a6c03.png)

Swamp trees now no longer come in oak wood, they now use a new set of wood called Purple Heart. This wood has a desaturated purple colour that will fit any spooky house build.

![Belladonna Flower](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/ec9b66383b3c9691f594a57b86af5cae2e49f31d.png)

The Belladonna, a new species of flower that poisons you when you touch it. Though it makes for a great source of purple dye.

![Swamp Reeds](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c0a3a8a9fcd1fcd65c5e487ef048c9bf00f3caee.png)

Swamp Reeds, an aquatic plant that generates 1 block deep in water. You can plant it anywhere on both land and sea while also allowing for a new way of obtaining brown dye.

![Firefly Bush](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/ecee507635d3eb61bf9c91b8a53a94196672a5d4_0.webp)

Fireflies bushes have been backported from the upcoming Spring Drop and expanded upon. You can use a bottle on them to aquire a Firefly Bottle, a new type of lamp that can be opened to emit the firefly particles without the need for a bush nearby.

![Sinking Mud](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/1a31a56ad4e1746d8d7dae15ca2c4bd30de12d8d.png)

A new type of mud can now be found generating in Swamps, Sinking Mud! This waterloggable block causes mobs to sink into it unless their wearing leather boots or are native to swamps.

Two new structures can also be found in the new swamps to give you something to look for while trekking through the plants and mud.

![Swamp Ruins](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/21885e772af501c79ecbb420b89465f65914e780.png)

Swamp Ruins are a new archeology structure, they contain various pieces of swamp themed loot. But hidden away under the rubble is the Frog Mask. This new armour piece has a unique usage, to direct you to the mysterious Swamp Temp- why do I hear giggling?

![Imp Village 1](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/d0ee7d7b49acd299b513b3d9a746182625f77945.png)

The Frog Mask was _supposed_ to lead you to a frog themed temple, but it seems our newest additions have made themselves at home in this sacred place.

![Imp Village 2](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/1c59c4f7fde54741653ee574bfc1ab843a7f8a91.png)

Meet the Impillagers, the stars of the mod. These tricky critters will provide trades for a random assortment of items based on their profession, but be careful, these guys got kicked out of the Nether for their scams which they are now trying to pull on the Overworld.

![Imp Professions](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/bd37b181e40e8096f6146a11b4ee30d1c836e29d.png)

If you can sort through the scams, however, there is an abundent of items at a range of usefulness.
